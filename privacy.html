Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. Your privacy is respected and protected.

1. Overview

At Mini Game Arcade, accessible from our website, the privacy of our visitors is of paramount importance to us. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is or is not collected and recorded by Mini Game Arcade and how we use it.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not require user accounts, email registration, passwords, or personal identifying information to play games on this website. All gameplay happens entirely client-side within your web browser.

3. Browser Local Storage

Our games use modern browser localStorage solely to improve your gaming experience. Specifically, we store:

  • High Scores: Your best score for each game mode (e.g. fruit_slice_hs_arcade, chroma_breaker_hs) so you can track your personal records.
  • Sound Preferences: Whether you have muted or unmuted the in-game audio synthesizer.
  • Tutorial Flags: Whether you have already viewed the first-time how-to-play instruction overlay.

This data is stored purely on your local device and is never transmitted to any external server or third party. You can clear this data at any time by clearing your browser cache.

4. Hosting & Log Files

Mini Game Arcade is hosted as a static website on Vercel. Like most web hosting platforms, standard server logs (such as IP addresses, browser types, referring pages, and date/time stamps) may be generated for operational security, DDOS prevention, and performance diagnostics. These logs do not contain personally identifiable information.

5. Advertising Partners

If advertising networks (such as Google AdSense) are enabled on this site in the future, these third-party vendors may use cookies or web beacons to serve ads based on your visit to this and other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings or www.aboutads.info.

6. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. Our games contain no chat systems, user profiling, or invasive tracking.

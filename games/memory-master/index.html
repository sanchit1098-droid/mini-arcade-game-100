HOW TO PLAY

1. MEMORIZE At the start of every level, all gems are revealed face-up for 3–5 seconds with an active countdown. Memorize where identical crystals lie!
2. MATCH PAIRS Once the cards flip face-down, select two cards (click, tap, or use Tab / Arrows + Enter / Space). Matched pairs stay revealed permanently!
3. NO PENALTY RESTART If a pair doesn't match, they flip back face-down after 1 second. The round continues without restarting—keep hunting!
4. COMBO STREAKS Find consecutive matching pairs without a miss to ramp up your score multiplier from x1.5 up to x3.0!
5. ESCALATING BOARDS Conquer expanding grids from 3×4 (6 pairs) up to 6×8 (24 pairs) with tighter memorization windows!
